# Addicted to Loving You: The Paranoid and Domineering Boss's Convicted Wife

Book ID: 21000001691; assignment 3.

Read coverage: official synopsis and all 16 supplied free chapter files, complete contents. This does not mean the complete published novel was read.

Supplied genre: ['Romance']; tags: Lawyer, CEO, Revenge, Drama, Eastern, Betrayal.

The 16 supplied files cover narrative chapters 1–17; file 006 combines chapters 6 and 7 and repeats the chapter-7 episode multiple times. All repeated material was viewed, but repetition must not be interpreted as multiple plot developments. Yiran's prison torture and permanent hand damage establish the cost of a supposedly wrongful conviction for killing Yi Jinli's fiancée. Released and working sanitation, she is attacked, saved by a shabby stranger, and offers him shelter because she recognizes his loneliness. The reader promptly learns he is Yi himself. She names him a younger brother and spends scarce earnings on clothing and a phone; he treats the concealment as a game, then becomes increasingly attached through reciprocal warmth, meals and care during a nightmare/stomach illness.

External humiliations escalate from family exploitation to attempted sexual exploitation for a sister's acting role, workplace/classmate ridicule, and a coerced reunion ending in assault and near drowning. Yiran repeatedly refuses false debt, apologizing for others' violence, and being sold for the mother's photo album. Her choices have material and bodily costs; neither a lost law license nor trauma can be fixed by a confident speech. Qin Lianyi supplies a distinct support relationship: years of visits, case investigation, career sacrifice and direct warning about trusting a stranger. Yi grows jealous even of this friendship. He secretly punishes an attacker and destroys a boastful woman's car, but also intentionally lets situations proceed as a test and initially ignores an accident to prove detachment. His care is therefore compromised, not evidence of an already safe relationship.

The chapter-17 free endpoint brings Yi into public view while rescuing Yiran from former classmates before her abusive ex and his fiancée; it stops before Yiran's understanding of his identity or the case truth is resolved. Emotional investment is both outrage at injustice and a very concrete fantasy of being awaited at home. The heroine is initially the giver of care, despite the man's hidden wealth. Lawyer is a lost identity and a possible exoneration plot, CEO is concealed structural power, Revenge includes both punishment of her and his covert retaliation, Betrayal includes family/ex and the still-hidden deception, Eastern corresponds to explicit Chinese setting and social/monetary detail. The synopsis leaps ahead to romance, case revelation, flight and reversed pleading; none of those later stages is verified by the free body. This opening's healing-companionship mechanism cannot be collapsed into a standard rich rescuer courtship, nor its violence into a generic 'misunderstanding'.
