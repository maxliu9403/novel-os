## Conflict sample — not canon

Christian set the drawings on the kitchen table, nudging Olivia's pencil cup aside.

“I need another pair of eyes.”

“For the bid?”

“Tonight, if you can. Then a little consulting once we get it.”

The roll settled against my mug. I moved the mug and reached for the rubber band before I had answered.

“What would you need me to do?”

“You're good at this. You'd spot things I'd miss.”

“That isn't what I asked.”

He pulled out a chair. There wasn't room to pass behind it without turning sideways.

“We can work out the details.”

“I'm starting a new job.”

“I know. I'm asking for a little help.”

I let go of the rubber band.

“I can't review it. Or be your consultant.”

“Evelyn.”

I put the pencil cup back where Olivia could reach it.

“You'll have to ask someone else.”

## Quiet sample — not canon

“The moon goes underneath,” Olivia said.

I had put the cardboard circle on top of the puppets.

“Sorry.”

“It'll squash their hats.”

I lifted it out. One hat had already come loose. I reached for the glue, then waited.

“Do you want me to—”

“Just hold it.”

I pressed the edge where she showed me. She squeezed out more glue than we needed and caught the drop with a scrap of paper.

“I heard everyone liked it,” I said.

“Maya did the loud bit wrong.”

I smiled.

“It was still funny,” she added, looking at the hat.

I wanted to ask her to do the loud bit. Instead, I kept my finger down. The glue felt cool through the thin paper.

After a while she said, “You can let go now.”

The hat stayed. We found a place for the moon along the side of the box.
