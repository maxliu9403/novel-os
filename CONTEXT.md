# Novel OS domain language

## Workshop next-step capabilities (deployed 2026-11-12)

No pending major choice does not mean author-confirmed length. The read-only `next_step` projection separates discussion, capacity planning/selection, delivery and recovery. Typed `advance` actions are idempotent control intent, not adoption of model proposals; only their `author_note` is author testimony. Legacy messages retain their prior interpretation. Paused or failed operations keep their existing recovery path.

Capacity citations may use source context and evidence actually supplied by the successful request, bound by a host-owned receipt to the session revision and source packet. Citation validity establishes provenance, not author approval or literary quality. Failed candidates and structured diagnostics remain available for the next explicit refinement; historical capacity fingerprints stay unchanged. See [implementation verification](docs/reports/2026-11-12-workshop-next-step.md).

## Source request execution and recovery (deployed 2026-11-12)

`SourceRequestSession` is the shared boundary for whole-story design and portable source-evidence requests. It preserves legacy receipt formats and complete immutable input, while each actual execution has an opt-in `CompletionAttempt` with private output and safe diagnostics. Local timeout/cancellation, remote outcome, complete response, and business completion are distinct facts. Parseable partial text and a stopped local child never prove complete generation.

Verified saved completions resume without model calls. Unknown requests require a receipt-bound, idempotent explicit re-execution when no result can be retrieved; old receipt bytes and attempt evidence remain. Current model settings apply to the new attempt without rewriting original inputs. Missing/corrupt completed receipts fail closed. Route UI capabilities and job results follow these distinctions; read-only status never sends or migrates. Existing novel jobs and historical source artifacts remain untouched. See [recovery implementation](docs/reports/2026-11-12-source-request-recovery.md).

## Source design status (deployed 2026-11-12)

The source library's optional `design` projection is distinct from legacy `study` reading status. It reports actual route-worker activity, saved steps and the currently relevant mode without loading story artifacts or modifying runs. Lists and route details share active-worker-first, then latest-update ordering; an orphaned persisted running state is interrupted, not live. UI filters and refresh preserve the current route mode and unsent feedback. See [implementation acceptance](docs/reports/2026-11-12-source-design-status.md).

## Whole-story source routes (implemented 2026-11-11; deployed 2026-11-12)

`source-story-design.v1` is an explicit new design operation beside legacy SourceStudy. It reads all supplied material and produces comparable whole-story proposals with concrete causality, emotional scenes, choices, costs, highlights, long promises and endings. A route is not a source chapter or an adopted skeleton. Author corrections, model assumptions, observed source events, character statements and synopsis promises retain separate authority. Reading coverage proves supplied input, not literary understanding.

`story-design` stores immutable source-understanding and route-set versions, exact feedback, scoped revision/expansion actions and model receipts. Source correction preserves historical observations and their provenance without treating a superseded interpretation as a current obligation. Capacity handling keeps complete evidence and artifacts; a changed candidate requires renewed earlier coverage. Indivisible required information that still exceeds capacity is preserved and reported, never silently removed.

`source-route-context.v1` binds a selected discussion direction, actual editable Genre/Tags, latest author feedback and a complete `source-route-evidence.v1` private packet. Selection establishes only the direction node; proposed events remain editable. A durable handoff freezes types, source evidence, cover and discussion rules before creating a session and its first turn. `creative-handoff.v5` carries this binding into subsequent creation. Existing discussions and running novels keep their original contracts. See [implementation and validation](docs/reports/2026-11-11-source-story-routes.md).

## Residual outline correction (2026-11-11)

A terminal candidate is the last verified correction paired with its exact blocked review. Residual correction continues that lineage with source-authorized narrative edits and typed promise operations. Affected chapters and read-only dependencies do not grant writes. Progress controls further attempts, while the complete semantic review controls publication. Every completed request retains its original source view and partition when connection capacity changes. See [resumable convergence](docs/design/2026-11-11-resumable-outline-convergence.md).

Existing role metadata has its own source-bound correction operation. Stable IDs remain unchanged; pronoun usage and gender are independently evidenced. `identity_constraints` records that two role occurrences have an unknown identity relation, with original evidence for each. It cannot merge identities or transfer knowledge, injuries or fate. These constraints persist in the story bible and canon hash and remain complete in every writing/review context, including soft-budget and fallback paths. Absent legacy constraints add no default canon fields. Exact empty candidate fields may be cited as empty; author quotations must remain nonblank. Source silence does not forbid a compatible planned transition, and a generated bridge must not be presented as author testimony.

## Planning evidence and author decisions (2026-11-10)

Author evidence is exact text from the adopted source. Candidate evidence identifies a generated field and its exact text; it is evidence about the draft, not a new source fact. Affected chapters describe consequences, writable chapters grant scoped changes, and dependencies remain read-only. Independent adjudication distinguishes candidate drift, compatible interpretations and two irreconcilable author passages. A verified author choice binds the run, source and unordered pair of passages across model changes. Every applicable choice must reach publication review. See [planning authority](docs/design/2026-11-10-source-authority-in-planning.md).

- **Full-book execution observation**: public request/activity metadata belonging to a specific run execution. Worker heartbeat, transport events, observed content and a complete reply are different signals; none implies uninterrupted remote reasoning.

## Creation workspace lifecycle

- **Cover progress**: a concise projection of active tasks, usable directions, candidate quality and explicit selection. It is separate from manuscript completion and does not grant cover approval.
- **Explicit cover batch**: one deliberate generation request reusing a still-valid approved direction, identified by a durable owner-scoped request ID and a separate server task reservation. Another deliberate request can create another batch after completion or failure; the prior batch, spent attempts and costs remain recorded. Replaying the same request or a coalesced active-task alias returns the original task, even after termination or restart. Refresh never authorizes new images, and single-candidate repair approvals do not grant full-batch permission.
- **Single-candidate repair**: a source-bound revision of one candidate. Explicit approval covers exactly one new image call, or zero for local recomposition/evaluation. The result is saved in a new batch with retained-candidate lineage; the parent batch and official selection remain unchanged.
- **Reference-image refinement**: a user-requested edit of an existing candidate, with the requested changes and original image hash bound to a reviewed single-candidate direction. Approval permits one image edit using the actual reference bytes. Composed covers edit the retained text-free artwork and recompose the approved title. Fresh image review and explicit selection are required; parent and sibling images remain intact.
- **Refinement retry**: an explicit recovery from a failed or interrupted refinement. A still-valid reviewed proposal can be copied into a new awaiting-approval task without another planning call; the original reference and request stay bound. The user must approve one new image edit. Duplicate retry requests return the same successor, and another failure is retried from that successor. Old attempts, consumed allowances and costs remain recorded. A saved edited image is reviewed or recomposed instead of being generated again; an outdated proposal requires explicit replanning.
- **Restarted brainstorm round**: an explicitly confirmed fresh derivation from edited basics. Earlier discussion and skeletons remain archived, while a revision boundary excludes them from the new model context and candidate fallback. Usage history remains attributable to the same discussion.
- **Workshop turn intent**: `discuss` is the default operation, including route choices, answers and further exploration with an existing skeleton. It returns phased story proposals and retains the saved skeleton; model output cannot promote the turn into delivery. The author's separate Generate/Update Complete Skeleton action sends `deliver`, including any unsent instructions, without an extra confirmation or a minimum round count. Intent persists through pause/retry; a verified legacy delivery checkpoint can still be explicitly resumed.
- **Skeleton delivery checkpoint**: a complete saved story design, chapter plan, or assembled skeleton, bound to one discussion input and Skill version. Fresh explicit delivery starts with actual story design, choosing and freezing the narrative mode in that response rather than spending a separate automatic-handoff call. Explicit recovery reuses saved stages and regenerates only unfinished work; it does not revive a terminated remote model request. The assembled result must pass the full existing validation before replacing the formal skeleton.
- **Assembly part**: one complete, validated supplement to saved story and chapter plans. Six bounded parts provide publication settings, relationships/world rules, secrets/payoffs, reading/style guidance, cover handoff, and delivery review. The application assembles the final 17 sections locally without requesting another transcription of the existing chapters. Corrections append a part revision; earlier artifacts and their hashes remain intact. Legacy v1 checkpoints can acquire parts during explicit continuation.
- **Delivery time budget**: each model request retains the configured workshop timeout; a staged delivery has a fixed total allowance of three times that timeout from the original turn start, including waiting and storage. Format repairs share that total and the existing two-repair allowance. Activity does not extend either deadline; timeout stops without automatically replaying a request. Ordinary discussion and existing-skeleton edits retain their original turn budget.
- **Observed model activity**: counts of public summary or text deltas from a native Codex metric batch. Receipt time is a batch observation, not an exact token timestamp. Connection metadata, prewarming, worker heartbeats and token billing are separate signals and do not prove ongoing model generation.

## 通用书名设计（2026-11-08）

- **命名资料投影 `title-context.v1`**：从已确认骨架选择命名必要的完整段落，不摘要改写源文，不把整个逐章规划和创作知识重复发给模型。保留作者全部命名要求；结局/伏笔回收用于防止虚假承诺与重大剧透。
- **能力提示词**：独立于 Genre/Tag 的工作区创作方法。首个能力为 `title-design`；自由 Markdown 编辑、追加版本和显式恢复，使用时冻结内容/hash；不允许编辑输出合同与工程状态规则。
- **候选与采用**：推荐固定五项，但切面/句式按故事自由选择；`angle` 为中文切面，`reason` 解释冲突与情绪，`source_ids` 引用投影资料。有效引用不构成语义或销量证明。候选产生不等于作者采用；确认后才修改书名与对应交付元数据。
- **直白命名 v2**：面向偏好直白叙事的成熟读者，默认英文8–15词、中文10–15字为软目标；候选硬上限15（汉字逐字、其他文字按词，混排合计）。不设硬最小长度，不截断，不影响手动书名或旧候选。直白、冲突和情绪仍由创作提示及作者验收，不能用长度校验代替文学判断。
- **按格式确认书名**：`story-first.v1` 无旧封面交接块是合法设计；采用书名只改顶部Title（及确为原书名的H1），下游启动和封面工作室从已保存骨架/作品元数据读取。任何已存在的旧封面块仍需完整唯一并同步title；旧/未知格式不可用缺块回退掩盖错误。叙事合同中的系列总名不属于单本书名。

## 正文规划输入表示（2026-11-08）

- **planning-context.v1**：请求级无损去重，完全相同的工程合同共享一个位置；结构化写作资料的相同长段落保留原位置引用，定义只发送一次。原文件、自由Markdown、作者决定和冻结快照不改。
- **planning-review-windows.v1**：只在Gemini网关单次大纲核查超限时按容量分组；全局候选与章序保留，完整章对象及相邻章重叠，每组明确提供/未提供范围。先预检全组、再调用、逐组保存、全部覆盖后才汇总；不等于重新加入正文逐章阅读评议。

- **bounded-attachment-v1**：Novel OS 与本地 Gemini 网关协商的纯文本传输策略。普通请求保留128000字节上限，较长请求通过鉴权能力查询确认后可使用156000字节总容量；计入1024字节封装预留。完整文本上传一次，网关复核附件与短说明总量，同一登录账号/出口发送，不截断、不重试生成、不回退超长内联。能力声明、一次标记读取成功、全书理解和实际模型身份验证是不同证据。

- **章节来源定位 `chapter_locator`**：代码从完整、唯一编号的已确认骨架定位原章标题与字符区间，填补全局映射的空来源引用，细纲只继承同章已验证映射。定位是阅读入口，不是剧情符合性或文学质量证明；原文引述、故事核查与后续质量门仍独立执行。无法无歧义定位时不能补写引用。
- **分卷高潮位置**：硬合同保证高潮章号位于作者确认的卷范围；末尾35%的常用位置只是节奏建议。早高潮后的余波与回报可进入质量提示，不能以格式检查为由搬动原故事事件。
