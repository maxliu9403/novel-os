# Commercial Cover Direction

The output is an original mobile-first serialized-fiction cover, not an imitation of a published cover or artist.

## Visual hierarchy

1. The exact title is readable at feed-thumbnail size and appears exactly once.
2. One compelling focal idea carries the story core: a person, relationship, meaningful object or environment.
3. Genre, emotional promise or a relationship contrast gives the reader a truthful sense of the story.
4. One subordinate signal supports a key secondary task; it never competes with the focal action.
5. The fictional world appears through setting, costume, objects, era, light, or social pressure rather than real landmarks.

## Four-expression portfolio

The default V11 medium is high-quality live-action film campaign photography.
Do not substitute oil painting, gouache, illustration, paper relief or sculpted
people. Use natural mature skin, credible light, clear eyes and hands, and clean
color separation; avoid muddy sepia grading or an indiscriminate aged appearance.
Keep title typography editorial and readable rather than making human figures
stand on giant sculpted letters.

Treat a four-cover batch as four different visual arguments for the same book.
The following are examples, not mandatory slots or a fixed composition sequence:

1. **Intimate character window** — asymmetric close plane; private reckoning; tactile editorial portrait; wounded recognition; airy literary serif.
2. **Relationship geometry** — triangular deep-focus tableau; direct relationship choice; prestige-drama campaign still; divided loyalty; fractured editorial serif.
3. **Evidence mystery** — evidence-led negative space; discovery beat; crisp editorial suspense; shock and dread; condensed evidence lockup.
4. **Kinetic threshold** — diagonal medium-wide motion; irreversible refusal or departure; cinematic key art; cathartic resolve; bold cinematic serif.

An optional fifth direction may use architectural social pressure and public isolation. Each direction offers a different evidence-grounded visual interpretation; it need not freeze a literal event. A signature location may recur when the underlying scene and conflict expression differ. Changing crop, pose, palette, prop, lens, or camera angle around the same location-action tableau does not form a portfolio.

## Conflict tableau and emotional geography

For the relationship-geometry direction, translate synopsis-level conflict into one visible cause-and-consequence tableau. Choose spatial hierarchy for this specific relationship: a shared plane, threshold, lateral opposition, reflection or depth may carry the choice. Foreground consequence and background cause are one available technique, not the required template. Connect the planes through gaze, body direction, distance, touch, refusal, departure, or interrupted movement. The viewer should sense the relevant relationship or pressure; its exact off-frame cause may remain unstated. The intimate, evidence, threshold, and environmental directions use their independently designed composition grammar instead of repeating this ensemble tableau.

Emotional atmosphere must come from behavior and relationship geometry, not from a generic sad face, a color wash, or decorative symbolism. Choose performance for its emotional truth and visual appeal. Quiet portraits, aftermath and source-related objects are valid; do not invent a decisive gesture merely to prove agency. An opposing pair may show proximity, secrecy, exclusion, attention, or alignment only to the degree supported by story evidence. In `scene` mode use one continuous instant. In `campaign_composite` mode assign each evidence-bound layer a visual role and explicit relationship; never use unexplained panels, decorative face grids, or imply unsupported co-presence.

Use a portrait `2:3` canvas. Preserve the provider's native resolution rather than forcing a pixel count; the default request is `2048x3072`. Export covers as JPEG or PNG, preferring JPEG for the default delivery. Reserve stable title space, keep faces and decisive objects clear of typography, and avoid arbitrary collages, UI chrome, logos, watermarks, author lines, taglines, and subtitles. Validated campaign composites may use deliberate layering.

## English / United States market profile

When `language` or `market_scope` identifies English-language or United States / North American release, add this profile to every image prompt:

- **Casting and identity:** preserve only identity traits explicitly established by the approved story. Do not infer or invent ethnicity, nationality, or other identity traits from the release market. Keep faces, hair, wardrobe, and styling consistent with the story's stated character and setting facts.
- **Finish:** direct a premium US commercial fiction magazine cover and entertainment key art. Use editorial photography, cinematic production design, controlled color grading, realistic skin texture, and a polished campaign finish. Avoid stock-photo posing, generic AI portrait lighting, and flat character sheets.
- **Story signal:** convey the central relationship, pressure or emotional promise through a truthful scene or editorial expression. A portrait, meaningful object, juxtaposition or aftermath may carry the idea. Visible action, reaction, and stakes can help a literal scene, but are not mandatory in every cover; do not demand proof of an unseen cause or exact refusal.
- **Typography:** reserve a clean title-safe zone and use Western editorial typography: a premium display serif or refined modern grotesk selected for the genre, with deliberate hierarchy, precise kerning, restrained tracking, and strong thumbnail contrast. Render only the exact approved title; never add taglines or extra copy.

Do not apply this typography profile to a non-Western market unless the approved handoff explicitly requests it. Market profile changes art direction only; story facts, character identities, fictional locations, and the protagonist's agency remain authoritative.

## Distinct concept families

Choose four concepts by default (3–5 when explicitly configured or requested), with different focal scenes, camera distance, pressure geometry, palette, emotional temperature, photographic finish, and typography. An existing approved direction retains its original count. Example families, rather than a required taxonomy, include:

- `intimate_character_window`: an asymmetric, protagonist-dominant private reckoning;
- `relationship_geometry`: a layered relationship choice with causal cross-plane action;
- `evidence_mystery`: a protagonist-led evidence discovery with graphic negative space;
- `kinetic_threshold`: the irreversible refusal or departure frozen in directional motion;
- `social_pressure`: an optional public consequence shaped by architectural pressure.

Changing only color or pose is not distinct. Each concept should make a different truthful promise about the same book.

## Prompt quality gate

Every generation prompt names the audience, genre, core conflict, focal scene, composition, palette, fictional world signals, one secondary signal, emotional promise, title mode and title language. Native mode includes the exact title; composed mode generates text-free artwork and binds the exact title separately in the approved local layout. For English / United States market branches it also preserves only explicitly established identity traits and includes a premium US magazine/key-art finish, a coherent source-supported scene or explicitly related campaign layers, and Western editorial typography. Block forbidden real place names, extra copy, invented identity/core facts, unsupported literal events, unexecutable title geometry or prohibited spoilers. Treat competing focal points, subtle agency and weak thumbnail hierarchy as improvement advice, not factual failure. Editorial juxtaposition is not a claim of a literal meeting.

## Executable design priorities

Keep specific camera, key/fill/practical lighting, subject/background separation,
color relationships and title hierarchy in their dedicated scene fields. These
are protected by the compiler; do not bury them in long explanatory rationale.
Realism preserves age and everyday setting, not mandatory fatigue, dirt, global
sepia or flat documentary exposure. Deliver polished, motivated film campaign
photography with wardrobe and styling faithful to the character.

Use one continuous title lockup in exact order; English reads left-to-right, then
top-to-bottom. Vary scale, line breaks, alignment and negative space, not clause
order. A title split across people should never force competing reading paths.

Choose the cast and expression needed by each idea. No design must prove a complete causal relationship or exact protagonist choice unless the user explicitly requires it. An off-frame cause is compatible with an honest aftermath concept. Preserve the novel’s core meaning without adding people or gestures merely for plot compliance.

## Human performance, not displayed plot symbols

Direct one shootable instant in scene mode, or one physically coherent performance per composite layer. For each person, choose the immediate scene goal,
a single target of attention, a distinct reaction beat and a physically possible
primary action. Before/after events and character agency summaries explain the
story; they are not extra actions to perform simultaneously. Keep natural body
weight, joint angles, infant support where relevant, and credible hand/object
contact. Refusal need not be a raised palm; intensity need not be a grimace.

Let emotion appear through subtle asymmetric expressions, attentive listening,
interrupted movement and the distance between people. Do not mechanically assign
a standard micro-expression to every face. Supporting props may remain stored,
partially visible or naturally handled instead of displayed as a plot inventory.

Naturalness and campaign quality coexist: designed light and color, natural
focus falloff, soft highlight roll-off and age-true tonal variation. Keep crucial
faces/gestures legible without uniform pore-level sharpness, waxy smoothing,
glassy eyes or synthetic rim outlines. Do not add dirt, wrinkles, noise or blur
as a shortcut to realism. Review these visibly, separately from correct anatomy.

## Curiosity and two independent judgements

Specify a visible fact, an unexpected contradiction, the image feature that triggers curiosity, and the unresolved question. Ground the substantive story meaning in the approved analysis; ordinary marketing staging need not be literally narrated. Be honest about a portrait’s emotional promise and give weak or generic hooks actionable advice. Give each direction a different truthful visual argument.

Review what the artwork communicates before showing the reviewer the novel. Keep this neutral observation frozen. Then compare it to the story promise and separately judge aesthetic hierarchy, human performance, photographic finish and typography. More characters do not guarantee conflict; factual completeness does not guarantee a compelling poster. Missing observation or uncertain text contamination must remain visible in the report.

## Static-frame composition preflight

For each concept, first describe what a viewer can actually see without names,
title, document wording, inferred ownership or a before/after sequence. Consider whether its broad story promise is legible and appealing. Record useful
body orientation, eyeline, contact and distance in executable fields; do not try
to eliminate every compatible interpretation of the same still. No stock gesture is required: keep the
source-supported action, natural performance and each story's own visual language.

Judge the image at the configured thumbnail dimensions, with the approved title
region already occupying its space. If the story depends on a tiny finger motion,
signature, unseen document contents or an expression too small to read, recommend simpler staging or a broader truthful promise. This is advice unless
the design actually contradicts a source fact or violates an explicit constraint. Do not enlarge every face or
add extra actors as a universal repair. Keep the title ground coherent across all
lines and free of high-contrast object edges that compete with lettering.

Check the portfolio's visual argument, not only its layout labels. Different
chapters, panels or typography do not create distinct promises when every image
depends on the same ambiguous micro-action. Preserve book-level facts while
selecting different evidence-supported ways to communicate pressure and response.

For composites, explain how the viewer sees the boundary between independent
photographs or epochs. A shared table, floor or horizon must not accidentally
merge spaces declared separate in the source-bound design. Deliberate overlaps
remain valid when their approved relationship is clear; do not impose panels on
every composite or turn an editorial object enlargement into a new story prop.

## Commercial review policy

Use `cover-profiles.v11`, `cover-readability.v2` and the classified `cover-commercial-review.v1` protocol for new directions. Distinguish `literal_scene` from `editorial` expression; the latter allows source-related symbolism and juxtaposition without fabricating a literal event. Hard findings name a materially false claim or breached author/source constraint and its evidence. Marketing appeal, ambiguity, composition and thumbnail readability remain visible optional advice. Neither advice nor a low causal score automatically starts another model round. Retain legacy contracts on historical approvals.
